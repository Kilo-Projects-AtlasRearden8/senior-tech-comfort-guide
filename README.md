# Senior Tech Comfort Guide

A plain-English, large-print beginner manual for everyday technology — phones, email, video calls, passwords, text size, and scam avoidance — written for seniors who want clear, step-by-step checklists without jargon or condescension.

## About

This guide is published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026), founded by Dylan Asdale, registered nurse turned AI author. Asdale Dynamics makes beginner-friendly AI education, books, guides, and practical tools. The company's book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain technology "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

This guide follows that same style: warm, polished, tested, local-first, and free of secrets or personal data.

## What's inside

- `docs/index.html` — Welcome page and guide overview
- `docs/phones.html` — Making calls, contacts, voicemail
- `docs/email.html` — Sending, replying, attachments, inbox cleanup
- `docs/video-calls.html` — Zoom, FaceTime, Google Meet step by step
- `docs/passwords.html` — Strong passwords, password managers, two-step verification
- `docs/text-size.html` — Making text bigger on phones, tablets, and computers
- `docs/scam-avoidance.html` — Spotting phishing calls, texts, emails, and fake websites

Every page includes plain-English checklists you can work through at your own pace.

## How to use

This is a static HTML site. No installation, no internet required after download, and no tracking.

1. Open the `docs/` folder.
2. Open any `.html` file in your web browser (double-click, or right-click → Open With → your browser).
3. Print pages if you like — they are designed to print clearly in large print.

## Accessibility

- Large default text (18px body, larger headings)
- High-contrast colors
- Step-by-step numbered checklists
- Plain language throughout
