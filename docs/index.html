Welcome

This guide is for anyone who wants to feel more comfortable using everyday technology. No experience needed. No jargon. Each page walks you through one topic with a simple checklist you can follow at your own pace.

You can read this on a phone, tablet, or computer, or print any page to keep nearby. The text is large and the layout is simple on purpose.

What is in this guide

TopicWhat you will learn

EmailSending and replying to emails, adding attachments, cleaning up your inbox

PasswordsCreating strong passwords, using a password manager, adding two-step verification
Text SizeMaking text bigger and easier to read on any device
Scam AvoidanceSpotting fake calls, texts, emails, and websites before they trick you

How to use a checklist

  1. Read one step at a time.
  2. Do the step on your device.
  3. Check it off (mentally or on paper) when it feels right.
  4. Move to the next step only when you are ready.

There is no rush. You can come back to any page anytime.

A quick reminder

Never give your password, Social Security number, or bank information to someone who calls, texts, or emails you out of the blue. See Scam Avoidance for more.
