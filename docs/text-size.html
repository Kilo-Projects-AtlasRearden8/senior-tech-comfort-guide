Why bigger text helps

If text is hard to read, you can make it bigger on your phone, tablet, or computer. You do not need new glasses or new devices — just a few settings changes.

iPhone or iPad

Checklist: Make text bigger on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Display & Brightness.
  3. Tap Text Size.
  4. Drag the slider to the right until the text looks comfortable.
  5. Tap Back.

If the text is still too small, go back to Settings, tap Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text, turn on Larger Accessibility Sizes, and drag the slider further right.

Android phone or tablet

Checklist: Make text bigger on Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Display (names vary by brand).
  3. Tap Font size or Text size.
  4. Drag the slider to the right until the text looks comfortable.
  5. Tap Apply or Done.

You can also make everything on the screen larger by going to Settings > Display > Display size (or Screen zoom) and moving the slider.

Windows computer

Checklist: Make text bigger on Windows

  1. Click the Start button, then click Settings (the gear icon).
  2. Click Accessibility (or Ease of Access).
  3. Click Text size.
  4. Drag the slider to the right and click Apply.

To make everything bigger (including icons), go to Settings > System > Display and choose a larger percentage under Scale.

Mac

Checklist: Make text bigger on a Mac

  1. Click the Apple menu (top left) and choose System Settings (or System Preferences).
  2. Click Accessibility > Display.
  3. Move the Text size slider to the right.
  4. You can also increase the size of the sidebar icon text under Desktop & Dock.

In a web browser

You can make text bigger on any website without changing your device settings.

Checklist: Bigger text on any website

  1. Hold down the Ctrl key (Windows) or Command key (Mac).
  2. Press the plus (+) key to make text bigger.
  3. Press the minus (-) key to make it smaller.
  4. Press Ctrl or Command plus 0 (zero) to reset to normal size.

On a phone or tablet, pinch two fingers apart on the screen to zoom in, or pinch together to zoom out.

Tip

If you change the text size and something looks odd, you can always go back and move the slider back the other way. Nothing breaks permanently.
