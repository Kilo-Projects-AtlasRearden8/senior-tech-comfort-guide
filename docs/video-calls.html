Before your first video call

  • Make sure your device is charged or plugged in.
  • Find a quiet place with light on your face (a window in front of you works well).
  • Sit close enough to the camera that people can see your face clearly.
  • Test your speakers or headphones so you can hear others.

Joining a Zoom call

Checklist: Join a Zoom meeting

  1. Open the email or message with the Zoom invitation.
  2. Tap or click the blue Join Zoom Meeting link.
  3. If asked, download and open the Zoom app.
  4. Enter your name when asked.
  5. Tap Join with video (or Join without video if you prefer not to be seen).
  6. Tap Call using internet audio or Join audio so you can hear and speak.
  7. Wait for the host to let you in.

Joining a FaceTime call (iPhone or iPad)

Checklist: Answer a FaceTime call

  1. When your phone or tablet rings, tap the green Accept button.
  2. If you want to start a call, open the FaceTime app.
  3. Tap the plus (+) button and type the person's phone number or email address.
  4. Tap Video to start the call.
  5. Tap the red end call button to hang up.

Joining a Google Meet call

Checklist: Join a Google Meet meeting

  1. Open the email or message with the Google Meet invitation.
  2. Tap or click the Join Meet link.
  3. If asked, sign in with your Google account.
  4. Check that your camera and microphone are on (you can turn them off with the camera and microphone icons).
  5. Tap Join now.

During the call

  • Tap the microphone icon to mute yourself when you are not speaking. Tap it again to unmute.
  • Tap the camera icon to turn your video off or on.
  • Tap the red phone or hang-up icon to leave the call.

Tip

If you are nervous, ask a friend or family member to do a practice call with you first. There is no harm in trying.

Video call safety reminder

Only join video calls from people or organizations you know and trust. If a stranger sends you a meeting link and asks you to join, delete it. See Scam Avoidance.
