Making a phone call

Checklist: Make a call

  1. Find the Phone app on your home screen and tap it.
  2. Tap the keypad (numbers) icon.
  3. Type the phone number you want to call, including the area code.
  4. Tap the green call button (usually a phone receiver icon).
  5. When you are done talking, tap the red end call button to hang up.

Answering a call

Checklist: Answer a call

  1. When your phone rings, look at the screen.
  2. Slide the green answer button to the right, or tap Answer.
  3. Hold the phone to your ear and speak normally.
  4. Tap the red end call button when you are finished.

If you do not recognize the number, it is okay to let it go to voicemail. You can call back later if it is important.

Saving a contact

Checklist: Save a new contact

  1. Open the Contacts or Phone app.
  2. Tap the plus (+) button or Create contact.
  3. Type the person's name.
  4. Type their phone number.
  5. Tap Save or Done.

Next time they call, their name will show on your screen instead of just a number.

Checking voicemail

Checklist: Listen to voicemail

  1. Open the Phone app.
  2. Tap the Voicemail tab (usually at the bottom right).
  3. Tap a message to see it.
  4. Tap the play button (triangle icon) to listen.
  5. Hold the phone to your ear, or use speakerphone if it is easier to hear.

Text size on your phone

If the phone buttons or text are hard to see, see Text Size for instructions on making everything bigger.

Phone safety reminder

If someone calls you and asks for money, gift cards, passwords, or your Social Security number, hang up. It is almost certainly a scam. See Scam Avoidance.
