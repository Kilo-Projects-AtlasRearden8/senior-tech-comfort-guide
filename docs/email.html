Sending an email

Checklist: Send a new email

  1. Open your email app or website (for example Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, or Apple Mail).
  2. Tap or click Compose, New message, or a pencil icon.
  3. In the To box, type the person's email address (for example: name@example.com).
  4. In the Subject box, type a short topic (for example: "Hello from Maria").
  5. In the big box below, type your message.
  6. Tap or click Send.

Replying to an email

Checklist: Reply to a message

  1. Open the email you want to answer.
  2. Tap or click Reply (usually an arrow pointing left).
  3. Type your answer in the box that appears.
  4. Tap or click Send.

Use Reply all only if everyone who received the original message needs to see your answer.

Adding an attachment (photo or document)

Checklist: Attach a file

  1. Start a new email or open a reply.
  2. Look for a paperclip icon or a button that says Attach file or Insert photo.
  3. Tap or click it.
  4. Choose the photo or file you want to send from your device.
  5. Wait for it to attach, then tap or click Send.

Cleaning up your inbox

Checklist: Tidy your inbox

  1. Open your email app.
  2. Delete messages you no longer need: select a message, then tap the trash or delete icon.
  3. If you get unwanted emails from a company, look for an Unsubscribe link at the bottom of the email and tap it.
  4. Mark obvious junk as Spam or Junk so similar messages go there automatically.

Email safety reminder

Never click links or open attachments in emails from people you do not know. Scammers often pretend to be banks, delivery companies, or the government. See Scam Avoidance to learn what to look for.
